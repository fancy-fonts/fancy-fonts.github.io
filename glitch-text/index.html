How glitch text works

Unicode includes combining characters, small marks like accents that attach to the letter before them. Glitch text stacks many combining marks on every character until they overflow above and below the line, creating the distorted, corrupted look people call zalgo.

Pick the right intensity

  • Light keeps the word readable with a subtle distorted edge. Best for usernames and bios.
  • Medium is clearly corrupted but still guessable. Good for horror captions and comments.
  • Extreme buries the letters under heavy marks. Use it on one short word at a time.

Use it responsibly

A long wall of glitch text can make comment sections hard to scroll and can slow down old phones, and some servers remove it automatically. Short bursts get the reaction; walls of it just annoy people.

Frequently asked questions

What is zalgo text?

Zalgo text is normal text stacked with dozens of Unicode combining marks, the small accents meant to sit above or below a letter. Pile enough of them on one character and the marks spill over neighbouring lines, which produces the creepy glitch effect.

Where does glitch text work?

It works in most apps that accept Unicode: Discord, WhatsApp, Instagram comments, YouTube comments and many games. Some platforms strip combining characters, and heavy glitch text can break a layout, so the Light level is the safest choice.

Can glitch text crash an app?

Extreme zalgo with very long input can slow down older phones while rendering. It cannot damage anything, but use the Extreme level on short words only.

How do I remove the glitch effect?

Just retype the word in plain letters. Glitch text is ordinary characters, so deleting it and typing fresh text removes it completely.
